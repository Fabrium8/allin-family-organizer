Privacy Policy

All-in Family Organizer · Effective date: November 21, 2026

All-in Family Organizer (the "App") is published by Fabrium8 Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App handles and how. Contact: allinfamilyorganizer@gmail.com.

1. Summary

The App stores your family's information only on your own device. We do not operate servers that receive your content, we do not ask you to create an account, we do not show ads, and the App does not include advertising or tracking. The App's features work without an internet connection; internet access is used only to manage Premium purchases and subscriptions (see section 4). We do not sell your personal data.

2. Information you enter in the App

You may enter and store, for example: profiles of family members, including children (names, photos, allergies, school and activity information); documents and photos; expenses; tasks, events and reminders; lists; and notes. All of it is kept in the App's private storage on your device and is not sent to us or to any server operated by us.

The App is meant to be used by adults (parents or guardians), who are responsible for the information they enter about other people, including children. The App is not directed to children.

3. Permissions the App may request and why

  • Camera: to take photos and scan documents that you choose to save in the App.
  • Calendar (read only): to import events from your device calendar when you ask for it.
  • Notifications, exact alarms and start-up: to show your reminders and daily summary at the right time, and to schedule them again after the device restarts.
  • Fingerprint / face / PIN: to lock the App if you turn that option on. Biometric data is handled only by Android; the App never receives or stores it.
  • Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, nearby devices and location: only for the optional feature that syncs the phones of your family directly with each other (Google Nearby Connections). Android requires the location permission to discover nearby devices on some versions; the App does not read, store or send your location. Data travels directly between the devices, not through a server.
  • Internet: used only to check and manage Premium purchases and subscriptions. The content you enter in the App (profiles, documents, photos, expenses, etc.) is never sent over the internet by the App.
  • Voice dictation: when you dictate, the App uses the speech recognition service of your device (for example, Google's). That service is provided by your device and is governed by its own privacy policy.

4. Third-party components

  • Google ML Kit (text recognition and document scanner) runs on your device to read text from images and scan pages. The document scanner is delivered through Google Play services, which may download components for it.
  • Google Play: purchases of Premium are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (United States): a service provider that helps us verify and manage Premium purchases and subscriptions. See the next paragraph.

What RevenueCat receives: purchase history (which Premium product you bought and its status and dates), an anonymous identifier generated for your installation of the App, and your device locale and currency. It does not receive your name, email, location or any of the content you store in the App. The data is encrypted in transit and is used only to provide your Premium access and to understand purchases. Because RevenueCat is based in the United States, this data may be processed there. You can read RevenueCat's own policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

5. Sharing and backups

The App shares information only when you decide to, for example when you share a list, export a report or a calendar file, or create a backup file. In those cases the information goes to the app or location you choose, and that app or service handles it under its own terms. You are responsible for where you save and share your backups.

6. Retention and deletion

Your information stays on your device until you delete it in the App or uninstall the App, which removes the App's data from your device. Backup or export files that you saved elsewhere are not deleted automatically. Purchase data held by Google Play and RevenueCat is kept as long as needed to provide your Premium access and to meet legal obligations; you can ask us to delete the purchase data associated with your installation by writing to allinfamilyorganizer@gmail.com.

7. Security

Your data is stored in the App's private storage, and you can protect access with a PIN or biometrics. No system is completely secure, so please keep your device updated and protected.

8. Your rights

The content you enter stays on your device, so you control it directly inside the App: you can view, edit, export and delete it at any time. For purchase data, you can contact us to request access or deletion. If you are in Argentina, Law 25.326 on Personal Data Protection applies and the Agency for Access to Public Information (AAIP) is the authority in charge of enforcing it. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, the content you enter never leaves your device; the limited purchase data described in section 4 is processed by RevenueCat on our behalf. You can write to us at any time.

9. Children

The App is designed for parents and guardians and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If the App changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if cloud backup is added), we will update this page and the effective date above before the change is released.
